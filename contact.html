Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity and scope. Simple websites typically take 2-4 weeks, while complex applications can take 3-6 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

Do you provide ongoing support after project completion?

Yes, we offer comprehensive support packages including maintenance, updates, and 24/7 monitoring. Our support plans are tailored to your specific needs and budget.

What technologies do you work with?

We work with a wide range of modern technologies including React, Vue.js, Node.js, Python, React Native, Flutter, AWS, and more. We choose the best technology stack for each project.

Can you help with existing projects?

Absolutely! We can help improve, maintain, or completely redesign existing applications. We'll assess your current system and recommend the best approach forward.

What is your pricing structure?

We offer flexible pricing options including fixed-price projects, hourly rates, and retainer agreements. Pricing depends on project complexity, timeline, and requirements. Contact us for a detailed quote.
